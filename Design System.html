Design system.

The bones underneath Site v2 — colour tokens, type ladder, spacing scale, section patterns, and the components that recur across the page. Use this when extending the site (new pages, blog, snapshot tool, future Substack embed) so everything stays in the same family without re-deciding the same questions.

01

Colour — pulled from the photograph.

Four tones do almost all the work — and each is extracted from the headshot. The paper is the photo's sand backdrop. The ink is the shadow-side navy. The accent is the blue of the blazer itself. Nothing was invented; the palette was already in the picture.

Surfaces & ink

--paper · #E5DED1
Default surface. ~85% of canvas.
Paper deep
--paper-deep · #D8CFBD
Tonal shift blocks: impact areas, CTA, callout fills.

--paper-light · #EBE5DA
Card fill, optional. Subtle lift inside a Paper section.
Ink
--ink · #1F262F
All body text on light surfaces. Inversion block surface (Engagements, Footer).

Accents

Blazer blue
--blue · #5B6F82
The accent. Italics, mono labels, status dot, callout rule, button hover. Same blue as the blazer.
Blazer deep
--blue-deep · #3F5468
Active & visited links. The shadow-side navy from the photo. Rare.
Blazer pale
--blue-light · #8AA0B5
Reserved — a lifted version of blazer blue for future dark-surface needs. Not currently in active use on Site v2.
Ink scale
--ink-70 / 50 / 30
Body 70%, captions 50%, hairline glyphs 30%. Use the scale, don't invent new alphas.
Rule of thumb "If you're reaching for a fifth colour, you're solving the wrong problem. Make the existing four work harder before adding a new hue."
02

Type — one serif, one sans, one mono.

Three families, in clearly separate roles. Instrument Serif carries voice; Geist carries clarity; Geist Mono carries structure (labels, numbers, dates, codes, utility chrome). Never use mono for body, never use serif for nav.

04

Section patterns — three surfaces, used on purpose.

The page has two main surfaces — Paper and Paper-deep — used in alternation to give the page rhythm. Dark Ink appears only as a terminal block at the very bottom (the footer). Engagements gets the lift, not the inversion.

Paper · default

Paper · 85% of the page

Hero, Why Now, What Changes, About, "What I believe", Fit. Body text in --ink-70, headings in --ink. Hairline rules between blocks.

Paper deep · tonal shift

Paper deep · ~10% — the breath

Engagements section, CTA, and callout fills. The commercial heart of the page lives here — a subtle tonal lift that says "different beat, same world" without going dark. Pair with a 3px blazer-blue left rule for callouts and the snapshot card.

Ink · the footer

Ink · ~3% — the terminal block

Footer only. A small, quiet capstone at the bottom of the page. Mono uppercase glyphs at low alpha. Not used elsewhere — the page earns its weight through type and rhythm, not through inversion.

06

Brand & voice — what stays consistent everywhere.

A few non-visual rules that keep the site from drifting as it grows. Apply these to Substack copy, conference bios, the eventual snapshot tool, every email.

Mark

JB

Wordmark · "Jérôme Basdevant"

The full name in Instrument Serif. Never abbreviate to "Jerome" without the accent. The two-letter JB lockup is for compact spots only — favicon, mobile nav, footer.

Headshot

Used full-bleed in the hero column, never as a 220px circle avatar. The photo is the palette source: its sand backdrop, its shadow navy, and its blazer blue all become page tokens. Treat the headshot as canonical — never recropped, never recoloured.

Placeholder pattern

For any image we don't have yet (case logos, conference badges, future essay covers): a 135° stripe of paper / paper-deep. Never use stock photography or generated illustration.

Voice — what to keep, what to cut

  • Direct "What to kill." "Strategic deprecation."
  • Concrete "0 → 50 product & engineers." "Significant team growth."
  • First-person "I help leadership teams." "Our advisory practice helps."
  • Honest "You have a collection of experiments." "You have foundational AI initiatives in flight."
  • Quiet flex "25 years building products." "World-class technology leader."

When extending the site

Five-question test before adding anything new Does it answer a real question someone asks before booking? · Does it reuse an existing component? · Does it stay inside the three-surface system (Paper / Paper-deep / Ink)? · Does the copy survive read-aloud over coffee? · Does it earn its place, or just fill it?
